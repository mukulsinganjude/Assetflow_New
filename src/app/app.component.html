Admin

Full system access, including user management, employee records, consumables, and protected settings.

Data Entry

Can manage day-to-day inventory and handovers. Admin-only pages and settings remain restricted.

Viewer

Read-only access to available information; editing and operational actions are disabled.

A role can hide a tab or disable its actions. Some actions (such as deleting assets or managing employees) are admin-only even when Data Entry can use the page.

Working with records

  • • Search and filters only narrow what is displayed; they do not change or delete records.
  • • Before saving, check identifiers such as asset serial, Dell case ID, employee name, and CCIID.
  • • Use the template before importing spreadsheets and review any skipped rows or validation messages.
  • • Export when you need a spreadsheet copy of the records currently available on a page.
  • • If a tab or action is unavailable, ask an administrator to verify your role in User Roles.
